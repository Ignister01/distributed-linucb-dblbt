\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

Wi-Fi/NR-U coexistence schemes tune contention windows, reserve or defer
airtime, resolve mini-slot collisions, and schedule around technology-specific
timing \cite{naik2020next,saha2021survey,kosek2021downlink}. Deterministic and
semi-random WLAN backoff instead creates post-success structure without
explicit slot assignments \cite{he2013semirandom,sanabria2017higheff}. DB-LBT
extends this principle across Wi-Fi and NR-U: countdown interruptions estimate
active contention, and a deterministic recovery counter spaces later attempts
\cite{kosek2022dblbt}. Its TMC extension studies convergence with periodic
schedules and random busy slots, then evaluates traffic, interference, and
sensing perturbations \cite{tinnirello2026deterministic}. Both provide the
fixed mechanism on which our adaptive action space is built.

Machine learning has been applied throughout the IEEE 802.11 stack
\cite{szott2022wifiml}. LTE-LAA work adapts contention windows from observed
performance \cite{ali2017ml}, while reinforcement learning has optimized
technology-level efficiency and fairness \cite{han2020rl}. Our setting is
smaller and more constrained. A transmitter chooses among 24 protocol-valid
recovery profiles, observes only its own outcome, and must learn with limited
online samples. These properties favor a contextual bandit with an explicit
uncertainty bonus \cite{li2010linucb}. The contribution is the mapping from
local protocol measurements to this constrained decision problem, rather than
a new generic bandit algorithm.
