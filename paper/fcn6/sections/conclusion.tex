\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We formulated DB-LBT parameter adaptation as a protocol-constrained contextual
bandit driven by local MAC information. Under mutual sensing, reliable
isolated reception, local separability, and sufficient regime dwell time, the
11 measured features distinguish when a different recovery tuple can help.
Distributed LinUCB then selects among 24 legal profiles without changing native
Wi-Fi or NR-U access rules. The completed event study preserves the stable
DB-LBT regime and finds a small held-out gain, with the clearest contextual
benefit under combined dynamic load. Packet-level directions are mixed.
Longer ns-3 campaigns with controlled fading, sensing, and adaptation
timescales are the next step.
